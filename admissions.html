Important information

  • Admissions are handled at the school.Please visit or contact the office to get started.
  • Admission depends on availability.Class placement is confirmed by the school.
  • Bring the required records.Our team can advise if additional documents are needed.
  • Uniform and school guidance.Families receive practical details during enrollment.

Documents to bring

Please bring copies when you visit the school office:

  • ▤Child’s B-Form or birth certificate
  • ▣Parent or guardian CNIC
  • ▧Recent passport-size photographs
  • ▤Previous school record, if applicable
Our school office will confirm the complete document checklist for your child’s class.
