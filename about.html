OUR SCHOOL

Helping every student move forward.

Paragon Public High School serves students and families in Adda Gamber, Okara. Our school is a project of Punjab Education Foundation (PEF), Government of Punjab.

We believe a good education combines strong teaching with care, discipline and respect. Our goal is to give every child a firm academic foundation and the confidence to shape a brighter future.
